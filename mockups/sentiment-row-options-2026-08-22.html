What Reezer AI will be asked for, per leader

now — buy · hold · reduce · sell · neutral (what he'd do with a position today)

next — buy · sell · none (the move he's setting up for)

trigger — the one condition that flips now into next, in his words, with the level if he gives one

conviction — 0–100, how firmly he committed; drives the bar in A

So the user-facing question "should I prepare to buy, hold or sell?" is answered in the row itself: the now word is what to do, the next word is what to prepare for, the grey line is when.
